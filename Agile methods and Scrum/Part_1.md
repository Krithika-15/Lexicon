# Agile & Scrum - Part 1

## Exercise 1
In Waterfall method, all the requirements are gathered first, then design is made, then built fully followed by testing and delivery. Because of this flow, building software often goes wrong in the following ways:

**1**. In the middle we can't modify any of the steps like adding or removing a requirement.

**2**. While testing at end something might be broken, so it takes lot of time.

## Exercise 4

- Only the Product Owner decides what goes into the Product Backlog and in what order.
- If it's urgent, the Product Owner and Developers can renegotiate the scope of the current sprint, pulling the new item in and pushing something of similar size out, but only if the Sprint Goal still makes sense afterwards.
- If it's not urgent, the feature is written as an item in the Product Backlog, the Product Owner puts it in order, and it's picked up at the next Sprint Planning. The current sprint is not changed.


## Exercise 5

- Sprint Review and Retrospective happen at the end of a sprint.
- Review first, then Retrospective after that.
- Review - shows what has been built and checks whether we built it right.
- Retrospective - looks at how the team worked during the sprint, and what to improve in the way we work.
- Who takes part:
  - Review: the whole Scrum Team (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers) plus stakeholders - the customer, users and managers.
  - Retrospective: only the Scrum Team.

## Exercise 9
### What is wrong ?
- Everyone stands facing the manager and reports what they did is wrong, as people talk to the manager instead of each other.
- It looks like monitoring (Dark Scrum) and makes the meeting long when each person explains their day in detail.
- The Daily Scrum taking 45 minutes is wrong and it happens because of the above two reasons.

### What I would suggest?
- The Daily Scrum actually belongs to the Developers and not to report to anyone.
- Need to go through the work items from Done backwards, instead of person by person.
- The conversation then becomes about the work rather than about individuals and the Daily Scrum naturally gets shorter to around 15 minutes.


## Exercise 10
- a) A team building a new mobile app

Scrum as it is planned ahead with features - login, etc., They can be written as stories and put in order before the sprint starts.

- b) An IT Support desk

Kanban as it is unannounced, all day from outside. It has small independent things. It required urgent attention. Can't wait for the Sprint

- c) A team fixing bugs in a webshop that is already live

Kanban as bugs on a live site arrive without warning, so they cannot be planned into a sprint before it starts. Kanban gives the team WIP limits, so they finish before starting something new.
